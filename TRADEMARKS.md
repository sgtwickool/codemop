This keeps the name meaning one thing: when someone sees CodeMop, it's this project.

## Fine without asking

- Saying that your project uses CodeMop, works with it, or was reviewed by it, including the
  "Reviewed by CodeMop" badge in the README
- Writing about CodeMop, reviewing it, comparing it, teaching it, with its name and logo
- Sharing unmodified copies of CodeMop, under its own name
- Forking it to change it, contribute back, or use it yourself

## Needs permission

- Naming a fork, product, service or company CodeMop, or anything that could be mistaken for it
  (Code Mop, CodeMopper, and so on)
- Using the logo or Moppy for your own fork, product or service
- Offering a modified CodeMop, or a hosted service built on it, under the CodeMop name
- Domain names, package names and social accounts with "codemop" in them
- Anything that suggests CodeMop or its author endorses you

If you fork CodeMop to make your own product, give it a new name and its own logo, and keep the
[NOTICE](NOTICE) file, as the licence requires. Saying it's "based on CodeMop" is fine.

To ask for permission, [open an issue](https://github.com/sgtwickool/codemop/issues).
